P.01

SoccerTwos: Trained RL Agents Playable Build

Unity ML-AgentsReinforcement LearningPPOSimulation
⌄
The Brief
A 2v2 soccer match where every player is an AI agent, trained with reinforcement learning inside Unity's ML-Agents SoccerTwos environment. No scripted behavior and no pathfinding shortcuts. Just Blue vs Orange, learning from scratch.
Technical Approach
Trained locally using the standard Unity ML-Agents PPO workflow (mlagents-learn) for 1,000,000 steps on an RTX 3070 Ti (Lenovo Legion laptop). This build is inference only, so you're watching the trained policy play, not training live.
Results
The agents started out moving randomly. By the end of the 1M-step run they were regularly intercepting the ball and defending their own goal.
Why It Matters for Serious Games
Training agents like this is a real technique used in defense and training simulators. This project reflects hands-on experience with that kind of RL pipeline.

P.02

Chroma Blast Final Year Project

Unity 6XR Interaction ToolkitMeta Quest 2/3Photon PUN2/FusionAI Navigation
⌄
The Brief
A multi-user, physics-based VR paintball simulator for Meta Quest 2 and 3, built with two teammates as our Final Year Project. Projectiles follow real trajectory physics based on velocity and impact instead of instant-hit shooting, so aiming and cover actually matter. Real-time multiplayer runs on Photon.
My Role
Team of 3. I worked on the physics-based projectile system, the Player vs AI mode, and the spatial UI.
Interaction & Systems Notes
The projectile system uses real velocity and impact physics so shots behave like actual paintballs, not instant hits. The AI opponent runs on a navigation mesh with its own decision logic, giving solo players something to genuinely train against instead of a scripted target. The spatial UI is built to stay readable without getting in the way during fast VR combat.
